Plumbing installation in Apple Valley, for upgrades and remodels

Installation work covers the plumbing that goes in when you replace a fixture, add a bathroom, move a laundry or upgrade a kitchen. Good installation is mostly hidden: correct shutoff valves, supply lines in good condition, a properly vented drain and a pressure test before anyone covers the wall.

We install toilets, sinks, faucets, showers and tubs, laundry boxes, dishwasher and ice maker lines, outdoor spigots, water softener connections and new shutoff valves. We also install garbage disposals, which our garbage disposal repair page explains in more depth.

Same-day appointments are available when schedules allow. We explain the scope, flag anything in the existing plumbing that needs attention and give the price before we start.

Installations we handle

  • Toilets, including new flanges when the old one is damaged, see toilet repair and installation.
  • Kitchen and bathroom faucets, with new supply lines and shutoffs.
  • Sinks and vanities, including drain and trap changes.
  • Showers and tubs, with valve and drain connections.
  • Appliance hookups such as dishwashers, ice makers and washers.
  • Shutoff valves, replacing stuck or leaking angle stops and main shutoffs.

What sits behind a clean installation

Old shutoff valves are a common surprise. A valve that has not turned in years may leak or fail when touched, so we plan to replace it. Corroded supply lines, undersized drains and missing vents are other findings that show up when a fixture comes out. We tell you what we find and explain the options without pushing work you do not need.

Remodel projects often involve a general contractor, electrician and tile setter, and we coordinate our part in the sequence. Plumbing that must go inside a wall is done before drywall closes it. Permit rules vary by job and town, and we explain what we know.

If supply lines are corroded across the house, repiping may be the better long-term answer.

Permits and rules for installation work in Apple Valley

California sets the plumbing code, and the Town of Apple Valley Building and Safety Division handles local permitting. Rules for specific jobs vary, and a simple fixture swap is treated differently from an added bathroom or a moved drain, so ask the Town before starting a larger project. In nearby Victorville and Hesperia, the city building departments apply their own processes.

Septic matters for additions. Town planning documents describe about 70 percent of development as septic, and adding a bathroom or a bedroom can increase the load on a septic system. Check with the Town and a septic professional before adding fixtures to a home on septic. Hard water also shapes fixture choice, since Liberty Utilities reports a 2022 average near 80 ppm, and scale clogs aerators and shortens the life of heating elements.

Installation work in California follows the California Plumbing Code, the state standard for fixtures, drains and venting. Permit rules vary by job and town.

Source: Town of Apple Valley planning documents and Liberty Utilities 2022 water quality data.

Choosing fixtures that work with your water

Fixtures with simple, replaceable cartridges are easier to maintain in hard water. Faucets with removable aerators can be cleaned with a vinegar soak. Our faucet repair page explains what to do when a faucet starts to drip.

What affects plumbing installation cost

  • Fixture grade. Basic and premium fixtures differ widely in price.
  • Rough-in work. New lines, drains and venting add labor.
  • Existing condition. Corroded shutoffs and old supply lines add scope.
  • Access. Slab floors, finished walls and tight cabinets take more time.
  • Permits. Larger jobs may need a permit and inspection.
