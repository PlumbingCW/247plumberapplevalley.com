Hydro Jetting a Sewer Line in Apple Valley, CA: What Sets the Price

Jetting quotes vary because the jobs vary. Here are the factors that move the price, what a fair quote should spell out and how to tell whether jetting is the right job in the first place.

By the Apple Valley 24/7 Plumbing teamPublished October 29, 20264 min read

Why two jetting quotes can look so different

Hydro jetting is not one fixed job. A short kitchen line reached from a clear cleanout is a different task from a long main line with limited access and a root problem. A fair quote starts with a look at the situation, and it names the things that will change the number.

No honest plumber can give an exact figure for a sewer line they have not seen. What they can do is explain how the price is built and tell you what would make it higher or lower. That is the standard we hold ourselves to, and it is the standard to look for in anyone you call.

If you are still deciding whether jetting is the right method, our hydro jetting page explains when it helps and when a cable is enough.

The factors that move a jetting price

  • Access. A clear cleanout is quick, while a line with no cleanout may need one installed first.
  • Line length and size. A long run to the street takes more time, and larger pipe needs different equipment.
  • What is in the pipe. Grease, scale and roots need different nozzles and more passes.
  • A camera look. An inspection before and after adds time and shows the result.
  • Pipe condition. A damaged line may need repair before jetting is safe.
  • Timing. After-hours work is staffed differently from a scheduled visit.

What a fair jetting quote should include

1
Where the work will happen. The cleanout location, the length of line to be cleaned and the type of blockage you described.
2
What method will be used. A cable, a jetter or both, and why.
3
Whether a camera is included. And whether you will see the footage.
4
What could change the price. For example, a collapsed section, a missing cleanout or roots that need cutting.

When jetting is worth paying for

Jetting earns its cost on lines with a repeating problem. If a cable clears the line and the clog returns in weeks, the pipe wall is coated, and a jetter that scours the full width of the pipe can break that cycle. Restaurants, homes with a grease habit and older lines with scale are the usual candidates.

It is poor value where the problem is a single object or a damaged pipe. A cable clears a dropped toy faster and with less water. And a cracked or collapsed pipe is not a jetting job at all, because high pressure can make damage worse. That is why a camera look comes first on our calls.

Sewer and septic in Apple Valley affect the quote

The Town of Apple Valley owns and operates the sewer collection system, and the wastewater is treated by the Victor Valley Wastewater Reclamation Authority in Victorville. Town planning documents describe about 30 percent of development as sewered and about 70 percent as septic.

For a quote, that means you should know your setup. A home on the sewer has a lateral line that jetting can clean up to the street connection. A home on septic has a line from the house to the tank, and the tank and drain field are separate systems that a septic professional handles. If a camera shows the blockage is in the Town's main, you can report it to the Town.

Source: Town of Apple Valley planning documents and wastewater system information.

Questions to ask any plumber before you agree

Ask what method they plan to use and why. Ask if a camera is included and if you can see the footage. Ask what is covered if they find damage, and what the next step and price would be. A plumber who explains instead of pressing is easier to trust.

Ask also about the guarantee on the work. A jetting job leaves the line clear for now, but it cannot promise the pipe will never clog again, since the cause may be roots or a sag. A good plumber says so and describes how to keep the line clear.

Insurance and your sewer line

A planned jetting visit is a maintenance cost, and most homeowner policies do not cover maintenance. A sudden sewage backup that damages the inside of the house may be treated differently, and some policies require a water backup endorsement. Check your policy before an emergency, not during one.

For restaurants and food businesses, a regular jetting schedule costs less than an emergency closure. Our commercial plumbing page explains how we handle after-hours calls. Homes in Victorville and Hesperia are served by the same line.
