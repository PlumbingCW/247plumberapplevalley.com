Before we arrive: if your water heater fails or leaks in Hesperia

These steps limit damage and keep you safe while a plumber is on the way. Work through them in order.

1

Shut off the cold water to the heater

Turn the valve on the cold inlet pipe at the top of the tank clockwise until it stops.

2

Turn off the power or the gas

Switch off the breaker for an electric heater, or turn the gas control to off for a gas one. If you smell gas, leave the home and call your gas utility from outside.

3

Do not open the relief valve

Hot water from it can scald, and it is a safety device, not a drain.

4

Call the 24/7 line

Winter nights and frozen pipes in Hesperia

The January low in Hesperia averages about 32F, and cold snaps can drop lower. Pipes in an unheated garage or on an outside wall are the first to freeze, and a frozen pipe that splits becomes a burst pipe repair call when it thaws. Insulate exposed lines before the cold arrives and know where the main shutoff is.

If a pipe has frozen but not burst, open the faucet slightly and warm the pipe gently with a hair dryer, never an open flame. If water starts to flow from a crack, close the main valve and call.
