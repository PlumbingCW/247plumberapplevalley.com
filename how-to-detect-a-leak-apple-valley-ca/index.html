How to Detect a Water Leak at Home in Apple Valley, CA

A hidden leak can run for weeks before you see a stain. These checks, most of them free, can tell you whether water is escaping and where to look next.

By the Apple Valley 24/7 Plumbing teamPublished October 29, 20264 min read

Why leaks hide, and why it matters

Most leaks are not dramatic. A pinhole in a copper line, a toilet flapper that seeps and a slab leak under a floor can all lose water for weeks. The first sign is often a bill that jumps or a floor that feels warm, not a puddle.

Finding a leak early is cheaper. Water damage grows by the day, mold needs only a damp spot and a leak under a slab can undermine flooring. A few simple checks sort out whether water is escaping and whether the source is inside or outside the house.

If you see water spraying or a ceiling bulging, skip the checks. Close the main valve and call. Our burst pipe repair page covers that case.

The meter test, step by step

1
Turn everything off. Close all taps, make sure the dishwasher, washer and ice maker are not running and that nobody flushes.
2
Read the meter. Open the meter box at the street. Note the number, or the position of the small leak indicator, a tiny dial or triangle that spins when water moves.
3
Wait and recheck. Wait about thirty minutes without using any water, then check the meter again.
4
Read the result. If the dial moved or the number changed, water is going somewhere. If not, a leak is unlikely, or intermittent.

Other quick checks that find common leaks

  • Toilet dye test. Put a few drops of food coloring in the tank, wait fifteen minutes without flushing and look for color in the bowl.
  • Under-sink check. Feel the supply lines, shutoffs and the trap for dampness.
  • Water heater look. Check around the base and the relief valve discharge pipe.
  • Walls and ceilings. Look for stains, bubbled paint or a musty smell.
  • Outdoor check. Look for a wet spot in the yard, a spigot that drips or sprinkler leaks.
  • Warm spots on a floor. A hot water line leak under a slab can warm the floor.

Reading the meter result

If the meter moved while everything was off, the leak could be inside the house, in the service line between the meter and the house or in an irrigation line. To split the two, close the main valve at the house and check the meter again. If it still moves, the leak is in the line between the meter and the house, and if it stops, the leak is inside.

A meter that moves very slowly is common with a toilet leak, which can lose a lot of water without a sound. A meter that spins fast is a bigger problem. Either way, you now know where to start, and a leak detection visit can pinpoint the spot without tearing into walls.

Leaks in Apple Valley homes: water, soil and weather

Apple Valley sits on gently sloping alluvial fans in the Mojave Desert, and soil movement and temperature swings stress buried lines. The Town reports winter lows in the low 20s and summer highs near 110F, and pipes expand and contract with those swings. A pipe that is not insulated in an attic or outside wall is especially likely to fail after a cold night.

Water quality plays a part too. Liberty Utilities reported 2022 hardness between 57 and 130 ppm, and the Town lists 11 water purveyors, each with its own system. Ask your purveyor who is responsible for the line on each side of the meter. For irrigation and yard lines, remember to call Dig Alert at 811 at least two working days before digging.

When to call a leak detection pro

Call when the meter test says water is escaping and you cannot find the source in a few minutes. Call when you suspect a slab or wall leak, when a floor is warm or a wall is wet. Call if your water bill has jumped and the checks come up empty.

A leak detection visit uses acoustic listening, thermal imaging and pressure testing to find a leak without opening walls. Our leak detection page explains the process, and slab leak repair covers leaks under a floor. We serve Victorville and Hesperia too.

Documenting for insurance and acting fast

Photograph any visible damage, write down the meter readings and save your water bills. Insurance often covers sudden water damage but not gradual leaks, so a clear record of when you found the problem matters. Ask your agent before repairs when you can, and keep receipts.

If the leak is under a slab and water is running, close the main valve and call. Dry out wet areas quickly, since mold can start within a couple of days. Fans and open windows help for a small spill, and a larger one needs a drying service.
