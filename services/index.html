How to choose the right service

If water or sewage is moving where it should not, start with the emergency pages. Close the main valve, stop using water in the house and call (760) 986-1999. If the job is planned, such as a new water heater, a new fixture or a camera inspection, the standard pages explain the steps and what changes the price.

Not sure which one fits? Describe what you see and hear, and we will say which service applies and what to do while you wait. Same-day appointments are available when schedules allow, and you hear the price before work starts.
