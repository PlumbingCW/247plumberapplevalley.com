Garbage disposal repair in Apple Valley, before the sink overflows

A broken disposal is more than an annoyance when it sits under a sink full of dishes, and a leak underneath can soak a cabinet in an evening. Our emergency garbage disposal repair line in Apple Valley, CA is open 24/7, so call (760) 986-1999 at any hour. Switch the disposal off at the wall switch, unplug it or turn off its breaker, and never put your hand into the drain, even when it looks stopped.

Disposal repair covers jams, motors that hum but do not turn, units that trip the breaker, leaks at the flange, hose or housing, and drain connections that clog back into the sink. Disposal installation covers replacing an old unit or adding a new one.

Same-day appointments are available when schedules allow. We explain the cause and the price before starting, and we say so when a replacement is more sensible than a repair.

Quick checks before you call, with the power off

1
Confirm the power is off. Flip the wall switch off and unplug the unit or switch off its breaker. Never reach inside with the power connected.
2
Look for the reset button. A small red button on the bottom of the unit trips when the motor overheats. Press it once after the unit cools.
3
Try the hex key. Most units have a socket on the underside that takes a hex key, and turning it back and forth can free a jam.
4
Check for foreign objects. Use tongs or pliers to remove items such as bottle caps, bones or a fork, with the power still off.

When a disposal problem needs a plumber

  • Water dripping from the bottom or side of the unit, which means a failed seal or crack.
  • A humming sound with no grinding that does not clear after the steps above.
  • A unit that trips the breaker every time you turn it on.
  • A sink that fills even though the disposal runs, pointing to a clog beyond it.
  • A burning smell or smoke, which is a reason to unplug at once and call.
  • A leak at the flange where the unit meets the sink, which can drip into the cabinet.

Why disposals jam, hum, leak and smell

Jams usually come from stringy vegetables, fruit pits, bones, coffee grounds in large quantities or a foreign object, and they leave the motor humming while the blades do not move. Clearing the jam and resetting the unit solves it, as long as the motor did not burn out.

Leaks come from three places: the sink flange seal, the drain hose connection and the unit body. A leak from the body usually means the housing has cracked or rusted, and replacement is the honest answer. Odors come from food film on the walls and baffle, and from a slow drain behind the unit. We clean what we can and tell you when the cause is a clog or a worn unit.

A disposal that works but leaves the sink draining slowly points to a clog farther along, and our drain cleaning page explains how we clear it.

Disposals and septic systems in Apple Valley

Town planning documents describe roughly 70 percent of Apple Valley development as septic, and a disposal sends extra solids into a septic tank. If your home is on septic, ask a septic professional how often to pump, and use the disposal sparingly, since fats, coffee grounds and fibrous scraps fill a tank faster.

Hard water adds a wrinkle. Liberty Utilities reports moderate hardness for most of the Town, and mineral scale can coat the inside of a disposal and the trap behind it. Running cold water while grinding and flushing for a few seconds after helps move waste out of the trap.

Disposal makers publish safety guidelines that start with turning off the power and never putting a hand into the unit. We follow the same rule on every call.

Disposal installation: choosing and fitting a new unit

Replacing a disposal is a half-day job for most sinks. The old unit comes off the mounting flange, the new one clips on, and we reconnect the drain, the dishwasher inlet if there is one and the power. If the sink has a dishwasher connection, a knockout plug inside the new unit needs to be removed first, a detail that is easy to miss.

Motor size is the main choice. A larger motor handles more waste and jams less, while a smaller one is fine for light use. Mounting style and sound insulation are the other options. If the sink has no electrical outlet or switch yet, we explain what is needed, and our plumbing installation page covers the broader scope of fixture and line work.

What affects garbage disposal repair and installation cost

  • Repair or replace. A jam cleared and a new installation are very different scopes.
  • Unit size and features. A larger motor, sound insulation or a continuous feed model costs more than a basic unit.
  • Electrical setup. A switch or outlet that needs to be added increases the scope.
  • Plumbing condition. A corroded trap or drain line may need replacement during the visit.
  • Timing. After-hours calls are staffed differently from a scheduled visit.
