Faucet repair in Apple Valley when water will not stop

Most faucet problems are a slow drip, but a few cannot wait: a faucet that will not shut off, a supply line that has burst under the sink or a shower valve that leaks inside the wall. Our emergency faucet repair line in Apple Valley, CA is open 24/7, so call (760) 986-1999 at any hour. Turn the valve beneath the sink clockwise to stop the water, and use the main shut-off if that valve is stuck.

Faucet repair covers kitchen and bathroom faucets, laundry and utility faucets, hose bibs and tub and shower valves. Most repairs replace a cartridge, washer, seat or O-ring, and the price depends on the parts and the access.

Same-day appointments are available when schedules allow. We explain the cause and the price before work starts, and we say so if the faucet is worn enough that replacement is the honest choice.

When a faucet problem cannot wait

  • A faucet that will not turn off and a stop valve that will not close.
  • A supply line that is spraying or dripping inside the vanity or sink cabinet.
  • A leak behind a shower or tub wall, shown by a wet wall, stained ceiling below or water at the base of the tub.
  • A hose bib that is spraying from the handle or the body.
  • A faucet that sprays sideways or gushes, usually a failed cartridge.
  • A cold-weather split at an outdoor spigot after a freeze.

Common faucet and fixture repairs

Dripping faucet

A worn washer, seat or O-ring in a compression faucet, or a worn cartridge in a single-handle faucet.

Leaky handle

A loose packing nut or a worn stem, which water wicks past when the handle is on.

Low flow at the spout

A clogged aerator or mineral buildup, a quick clean.

Kitchen sprayer problems

A cracked hose, a stuck diverter or a worn spray head.

Tub spout leak

A failed diverter or a worn spout seal that sends water to the shower head and the tub at once.

Shower valve leak

A worn cartridge or a failed valve body, sometimes behind the wall.

Burst or failed supply lines and shut-off valves

The small flexible line between the wall valve and the faucet is a common failure point. Braided lines can corrode from the inside, and plastic lines can crack at the nut. The shut-off valve itself, often called an angle stop, can seize after years without use, so it may not close when you need it.

Replacing a supply line is a short job. Replacing a stop valve is also straightforward if the pipe behind it is sound. We recommend installing a quarter-turn valve so the next emergency stops with a single twist, and checking the main valve while we are there, because a main that will not close is the worst case. If a line has been leaking inside a wall, leak detection can map the damage.

Shower and tub valve leaks

A shower valve is the mixing device behind the handle. A drip at the shower head after you turn it off, or water that will not get hot, usually means a worn cartridge, and cartridges are replaceable without opening the wall in most cases.

A leak inside the wall is different. A wet wall, a stained ceiling in the room below or a musty smell near the tub can mean a failed valve body or a pipe connection behind the tile. We test with a pressure check and a moisture meter before cutting access, and we explain the repair path first. Tub drain clogs and slow showers are a separate issue, and drain cleaning covers hair and soap buildup below the strainer.

Hard water, cartridges and cold nights in Apple Valley

Liberty Utilities reports moderately hard water for most of Apple Valley, with an average hardness near 80 ppm in its 2022 data and a range from 57 to 130 ppm. Minerals can build up in aerators and cartridges, so flushing the screen and replacing a worn cartridge are routine upkeep.

Outdoor spigots are the other local concern. The Town's profile lists winters with lows in the low 20s, which can freeze a hose bib and split the pipe behind it. Disconnect hoses before the first freeze, and cover the spigot on the coldest nights.

Faucets and fittings for drinking water are made to lead-content standards in the United States, so buy replacement cartridges and parts from the maker or a plumbing supplier. A no-name part from an unknown source may not meet them.

What affects faucet repair cost

  • Faucet brand and type. Common single-handle faucets use readily available cartridges, while specialty brands may need special-order parts.
  • Repair or replace. A cartridge swap and a full faucet installation differ in scope.
  • Valve condition. A seized stop valve adds work, and replacing it can prevent a future emergency.
  • Wall access. A shower valve behind tile requires careful access.
  • Timing. After-hours calls are staffed differently from a scheduled visit.
